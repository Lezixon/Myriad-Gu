FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers before the first configuration.

A concise guide to the boundaries that matter: permissions, privacy, media, cards and persistence.

Are all commands public?

The catalogue is visible, but sensitive actions are permission-checked. Moderation, configuration, backups, expression copying, bridges and staff controls are not open to ordinary members.

Does every response use a card?

Rich systems such as reminders, levels, events, giveaways, tickets, forms and relationship profiles use cards. Small confirmations remain compact so the channel does not become visually noisy.

Can I add my own pictures or GIFs?

Yes. Welcome cards, rank cards, reminders, events, giveaways, role panels, forms and automations accept optional PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF files up to 10 MB. Nothing is forced.

How do expressions and linked channels work?

Permitted emoji and stickers can be copied from a markup, ID or HTTPS source. Linked channels forward attached images, GIFs and stickers. A custom emoji that cannot be delivered is omitted instead of being replaced by the bot’s own expression.

Are direct messages or profile pictures forced?

No. Direct messages, member avatars, timestamps, footers, photographs and decorative styling are opt-in settings. Public cards do not silently attach a member picture.

How is saved data protected?

Guild state, reminders, XP, tickets and configuration remain in the private data directory. Keep the environment file and data directory out of public repositories and back them up before major updates.
